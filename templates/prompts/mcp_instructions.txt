Karkas keeps this project as a tree of blocks with explicit links; the cards (block.ksl) are the source of truth and the code follows them. Pick the path by the task: a bug or a small change starts with kk_locate "<words of the symptom>" (blocks, files, functions), then fix, kk_check and kk_run_tests - no schema change needed. A feature or a structural change follows the full method: read kk_guide once, kk_overview, kk_view <block>, propose cards with kk_propose_change and wait for approval, then contracts, tests and code. kk_view ends with the model line of the block: on another model, hand larger work on it to that subagent (karkas-<tier>) with a precise brief and review its diff. The project's CLAUDE.md carries the short guide.
