@view dsp level=1
purpose effect chain applied to frames
dsp.chain    leaf  run configured effects over frames in a fixed order  thr=main  ok
dsp.echo     leaf  feedback delay line                                  thr=main  ok
dsp.gain     leaf  multiply samples by gain                             thr=main  ok
dsp.lowpass  leaf  one-pole low-pass filter                             thr=main  ok
--- links
dsp.chain  -call->  dsp.gain:process
dsp.chain  -call->  dsp.lowpass:process
dsp.chain  -call->  dsp.echo:process
--- external
in   stream.ring:pop  -data->  dsp.chain:in  sync=lock_free thr=any->main
out  dsp.chain:out    -data->  mix:in
